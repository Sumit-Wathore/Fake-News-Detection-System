Government Announces New Economic Package to Boost Rural Employment

NEW DELHI: In a major move to address rural unemployment, the Central Government today announced a ₹50,000-crore economic package focused on creating jobs in agriculture and allied sectors. The package includes subsidies for small farmers, skill development programs, and infrastructure projects.

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman stated that the package will benefit over 10 million households across the country. "This is not just an economic measure, but a commitment to our rural economy and the backbone of our nation," she said during a press conference.
