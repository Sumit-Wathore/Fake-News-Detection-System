Prevention Tips

How to Spot Fake News

Check the Source

Verify the credibility of the website or social media account sharing the information.

Look for Evidence

Check if the article cites credible sources, studies, or expert opinions.

Check the Date

Ensure the information is current and not outdated or taken out of context.

Cross-Reference

Check if other reputable news sources are reporting the same information.

Verify Images

Use reverse image search to check if photos have been manipulated or taken out of context.

Watch for Bias

Be aware of your own biases and check if the content is trying to manipulate emotions.
